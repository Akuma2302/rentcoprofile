Questions

Before you join.

Is it safe to rent out my things?

Trust is built into RentCo: users and listings are verified, every member carries ratings, and payments are handled securely in the app. Optional protection and insurance cover is part of the plan for extra peace of mind.

What does it cost?

Listing is how you earn, not something you pay for upfront. RentCo takes a commission on each completed booking — for example, on a RM100 rental the owner receives RM85 and RentCo keeps RM15. That figure is illustrative and final pricing will be confirmed at launch.

What can I list?

Anything useful that sits idle: cameras and gear, sports equipment, outdoor and camping kit, electronics, tools, event equipment, fashion and more.

Do I need separate accounts to rent and to list?

No. One RentCo account lets you be both an owner and a renter.

Where is RentCo available?

We're starting with university communities in Malaysia and growing campus by campus, then out to wider communities and SMEs. Join the early access list and tell us where you are so we know where to open next.

We're a university, society or business. Can we work with you?

Yes. Choose "University / partner" or "Club / society" in the form below and we'll get in touch about partnerships, campus ambassadors and pickup points.
